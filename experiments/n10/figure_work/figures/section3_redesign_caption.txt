Readout trajectories on ten-node cycles. Columns show two D8L8 and two D8L6 examples. Node index k denotes the path-relative node f^k(s), not an absolute node identifier. Color indicates the fraction of top-1 predictions matching each node, evaluated over 512 held-out cycles and all ten starting nodes. Red dashed lines mark the loop where the final-only training loss is applied (8 for D8L8; 6 for D8L6). Examples are selected for visual clarity from twelve D8L8 and five D8L6 runs; all evaluation examples are retained.
